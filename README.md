## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a **Full Stack Developer at V4 Company**, focused on building software that improves real business operations.

My strongest work today sits at the intersection of **backend development, APIs, process automation and systems integration**. I use Python and automation tooling to turn manual or fragmented workflows into maintainable applications and reliable processes.

- 🐍 Build backend applications and internal tools with **Python**, especially **Flask**, with additional experience in **Django**
- 🔌 Create and consume **REST APIs**, webhooks and integrations between business systems
- ⚙️ Design **n8n** workflows connected to CRMs, APIs, databases and AI services
- 🤖 Build **AI-powered automations and agents** used inside operational and CRM flows
- 🗄️ Work primarily with **MySQL**, with experience in **PostgreSQL** and **MongoDB**
- 🐳 Deploy and maintain applications with **Docker**, **Linux** and **DigitalOcean**
- 🎨 Bring a background in **UI/UX and design** to the way I think about product usability and frontend experiences

> I like to understand the business problem first, then choose the simplest architecture that solves it well.
